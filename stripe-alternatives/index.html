What is a Merchant of Record (MoR)?

A Merchant of Record (MoR) is a legal entity that takes full responsibility for payment processing, tax collection, and compliance on behalf of your business. For SaaS companies and digital product sellers, this means offloading one of the most complex operational burdens: global sales tax.

Why SaaS Founders Need Independent MoR Solutions

When you sell software or digital products internationally, you’re legally required to collect and remit sales tax (VAT, GST, etc.) in dozens of jurisdictions. Each country has different rules, thresholds, and filing requirements. A MoR handles all of this for you.

❌ Without MoR (DIY)

  • Register for tax in 30+ countries
  • Track changing tax rates manually
  • File quarterly/monthly returns
  • Handle audits and disputes
  • Maintain compliance documentation

✅ With MoR (Automated)

  • MoR is the seller of record
  • Automatic tax calculation
  • MoR files all tax returns
  • MoR handles audits
  • 100% liability transfer

What is Platform Risk Mitigation?

Platform risk refers to the vulnerability of relying on a single payment provider. When your account gets frozen or terminated—often without warning—your entire revenue stream stops instantly. This isn’t just a “high-risk business” problem; it affects legitimate businesses across industries.

Why Mainstream Processors Flag Legitimate Businesses

Payment processors use automated risk models that often misclassify compliant businesses as “high-risk.” Common triggers include:

  • Dropshipping & E-commerce: High chargeback rates, even if legitimate
  • Digital Products: Intangible goods trigger fraud alerts
  • Consulting & Coaching: Advance payments for future services
  • Travel & Events: Long time gaps between payment and delivery
  • Subscription Models: Recurring billing patterns flagged as suspicious

The Backup Account Strategy

Smart businesses don’t wait for disaster. They build redundancy by maintaining accounts with multiple processors.

What is Cost Optimization?

For high-volume merchants processing $20M+ annually, payment processing fees become a significant operational expense. Most businesses start with blended pricing (e.g., Stripe’s 2.9% + $0.30), but this model hides the true cost structure.

Blended Pricing vs. Interchange++

Blended Pricing

2.9% + $0.30

A single flat rate that combines interchange fees, card network fees, and processor markup. Simple but expensive at scale.

  • ✓ Easy to understand
  • ✓ Predictable costs
  • ✗ Overpay on debit cards
  • ✗ No transparency

Interchange++

IC + 0.3% + $0.10

Transparent cost-plus model where you pay actual interchange rates + a small markup. Typically saves 30-50% for high-volume businesses.

  • ✓ True wholesale pricing
  • ✓ Save on debit/corporate cards
  • ✓ Full transparency
  • ✗ Requires volume ($20M+)
