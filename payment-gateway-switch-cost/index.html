This guide covers migrating between marketplace payment processors and gateways, not core banking payment-switch infrastructure. Use it to estimate the switch cost before you commit, then compare your ongoing costs against your current setup.

The true cost of switching payment gateways

A gateway switch requires balancing one-off migration expenses against ongoing operational savings. For marketplaces, moving involves complexities like seller re-onboarding, token portability, and parallel testing. A lower processing rate only makes financial sense if long-term savings outweigh these upfront migration costs.

The five switch-cost buckets

Engineering & API Integration

Rebuild checkout, split payments, payouts, refunds, webhooks, and test suites.

Hidden Trap: Edge cases like partial refunds, retry logic, and duplicate webhooks often double initial dev estimates.

Token Portability & Recurring Data

Verify whether stored payment methods can transfer seamlessly without forcing customer re-authorization.

Hidden Trap: Non-portable tokens force manual card re-entry, causing 15–30% buyer attrition on recurring volume.

Migrating off an MoR? Paddle, Lemon Squeezy, and similar MoRs typically can't export payment tokens — plan for re-card capture, churn buffer, and customer communication.

Reconciliation & Finance Ops

Map new settlement files, fee structures, dispute logs, and month-end close workflows.

Hidden Trap: Running two different settlement formats simultaneously strains finance teams during month-end close.

Parallel Run & Cutover

Route a small share of traffic to the new provider, compare authorization rates and settlements, then scale up gradually.

Hidden Trap: Dual-vendor overhead — two dashboards, two settlement cycles, and both providers' fees — can last 4–52 weeks.

Contract Lock-in & Exit Fees

Check minimum commitments, early-termination fees, and notice periods before pricing the move.

Hidden Trap: A contract that is cheap to sign but expensive to leave can wipe out the new provider's savings.

How to estimate your switch cost before committing

Static formula

Estimated switch cost = (Engineering + Finance/Ops hours) × Loaded day rate + Parallel-run months × Dual-vendor overhead + Contract exit fee + Reconciliation rework

A planning decision range, not a precise quote. Pressure-test each input before you present the number internally.

Three risk multipliers to check

  • +23–37%Timeline overrun buffer Edge cases and reconciliation delays; the calculator defaults to the 30% midpoint.
  • 15–30%Buyer attrition Non-portable tokens force manual card re-entry, losing recurring customers.
  • KYCMerchant relationship & KYC ownership Confirm whether seller onboarding, escrow, and dispute responsibility transfer after the switch.
