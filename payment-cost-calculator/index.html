How we model marketplace payment costs

We break marketplace payment costs into five layers—processing, FX conversion, seller payouts, per-account platform fees, and monthly minimums—and model the first four for our core platforms using verified public rate cards.

Modeling notes:

  • Processing is the fee charged on each transaction: a percentage of the charge amount plus a fixed per-transaction fee (estimated from your AOV).
  • FX applies only to the share of volume that actually requires currency conversion—by default your cross-border share, adjustable under Advanced assumptions.
  • Payout and platform-fee layers scale with your active seller count. Set the exact number in Advanced assumptions for precise per-account costs.
  • Airwallex rates depend on the entity you sign up with. Select your entity in the calculator. When you change the entity selector at the top of the page, the Airwallex entity follows the closest match until you override it manually inside the Airwallex card.
  • A note on Europe: EEA card pricing varies by card type (consumer vs. commercial). This version uses flat reference rates per platform; card-type splits are planned for a future version.
  • All rates come from public sources: Stripe public pricing, Airwallex entity pricing pages, and PayPal's public FX spread. Rates shown are standard public defaults for comparison, not custom quotes. High-volume platforms can usually negotiate lower rates with all three providers, so treat the output as a baseline for comparison rather than a quote.

Before you sign: marketplace payment gateway contract checklist

The calculator estimates what a provider costs per month—not what it costs to leave, or what breaks when you do. Confirm these 12 terms in writing before signing.

1. Exit & Lock-in Terms

  • Early termination fee (ETF): Is there a fixed penalty or remaining-minimum buyout if you leave early?
  • Auto-renewal window: Does the contract auto-renew for another 12–24 months unless you give 60–90 days' notice?
  • Service unbundling: If you switch payment processing, do you lose access to FX accounts, payouts, or tax reporting?

2. Data & Seller Portability

  • Buyer token export: Will the processor export PCI-compliant card tokens to a new gateway, or must buyers re-enter cards?
  • Seller KYC/KYB records: Can you transfer verified seller onboarding data, or must every seller re-verify identity?
  • Historical ledger access: How long after termination can your finance team download payout and dispute logs?

3. Pricing & FX Traps

  • Minimum monthly fee: Is the monthly floor waived during a 3–6 month ramp-up period, and does it reset monthly?
  • FX markup lock-in: Is your FX spread capped in the contract, or can the provider widen exchange markups mid-term?
  • Tiered rate triggers: Are volume discounts retroactive across the whole month, or applied only to incremental GTV?

4. Switching & Migration Overhead

  • Backend engineering: How many developer weeks are required to rebuild split-payment webhooks and ledger logic?
  • Reconciliation rework: How tightly are your ERP and seller tax forms (e.g., 1099-K / DAC7) coupled to this API?
  • Parallel run window: Will you need to route new sellers to the new gateway while winding down legacy payouts?

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from a seller fee calculator?

This tool models platform-side costs—the processing, FX, and payout fees your marketplace absorbs on total GTV. A seller fee calculator only estimates what an individual merchant pays on their own checkout, ignoring multi-party splits and cross-border payout markups.

Why don't you include Finix, Dwolla, or WePay?

These providers require a US-resident principal with a Social Security Number (SSN) for underwriting. Non-US founders cannot pass their compliance checks, so we exclude them to keep this shortlist actionable for international teams.

Why are Adyen, Mangopay, Nuvei, and Rapyd marked "Custom quote"?

Enterprise and regional split-payment processors price on Interchange++ or custom corridor tiers based on your GTV, industry risk (MCC), and entity location. Rather than displaying guessed averages, we keep them as unpriced shortlist items alongside our baseline models.

Why does my entity location change Stripe and Airwallex fees?

Stripe charges a 2% currency conversion fee for non-US entities (HK, SG, UK, EU, AU) compared to 1% for US entities, alongside different domestic card baselines. Airwallex prices by local contracting entity and uses a 0.5% FX markup on major currencies, which typically widens the cost gap on cross-border volume.

What does "conservative FX estimate" mean for PayPal?

PayPal's currency conversion spread typically ranges from 3% to 4% depending on the currency pair and account tier. The calculator uses 3% as a lower bound so the baseline comparison never overstates PayPal's cost.
